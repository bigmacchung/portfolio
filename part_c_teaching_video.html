Four stories behind a price change

Each one tells us something different.

Regular into regular

Quiet everyday repricing. Demand noise, inflation drift.

Regular into sale

A promotion begins. Signs, displays, marketing all flip on.

Sale into regular

The promotion ends. Shoppers who stocked up may stay home.

Test into test

A silent experiment. Only the price moves. Nothing else.

The punchline

The test line is flat. The promo line is steep.

Yellow sticker bias

The steep promo slope is not only price. It is price plus signs, displays, and attention. Promos overstate how much shoppers care about price.

Takeaway

What to remember.

Trust the tests

They isolate price. Clean elasticity.

Discount the promos

Their slope includes marketing, not just price.

Shelf matters

Always compare inside the same shelf.

Business impact

Use test data to set regular prices. Not promo data.
