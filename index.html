A/B Testing Tutor

An interactive learning tool I'm building to teach A/B testing concepts.

The A/B Testing Tutor is an interactive web-based companion for understanding the full lifecycle of an experiment — framing a hypothesis, sizing a sample, interpreting results, and avoiding common pitfalls like peeking and multiple comparisons. The goal is to give learners hands-on intuition through guided simulations and bite-sized explanations.
